# Compressor C-204 Specifications

## Details
- Asset Tag: C-204
- Type: Rotary Screw Compressor
- Power: 250 kW
- Capacity: 1000 CFM

## Operation Notes
- Startup requires minimum oil pressure of 2.5 bar.
- Auto-shutdown triggered if discharge temperature exceeds 110°C.

## Historical Issues
- C-204 has repeatedly failed due to overheating in the summer months.
- A recommended corrective action is upgrading the cooling fan.
