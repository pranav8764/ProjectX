# Turbine T-01 Operation Manual

## Technical Specifications
- Tag: T-01
- Type: Steam Turbine
- Output: 50 MW
- Speed: 3000 RPM

## Operating Parameters
- Inlet Steam Pressure: 100 bar
- Inlet Steam Temperature: 540°C
- Exhaust Pressure: 0.1 bar (absolute)

## Critical Warnings
- Do not exceed 3050 RPM under any circumstances. Over-speed trip must be tested monthly.
- Vibration monitoring system must be active during all operational phases.
