# Pump P-101 OEM Manual

## Equipment Specifications
- Asset Tag: P-101
- Asset Type: Centrifugal Pump
- Manufacturer: FlowServe
- Max Pressure: 150 psi
- Max Temperature: 120°C

## Maintenance Guidelines
- Check seals every 6 months.
- Replace bearings every 2 years.
- Lubricate weekly.

## Common Failures
- Seal leakage: Usually caused by excess pressure or worn-out O-rings.
- Vibration: Often due to misalignment or bearing wear.
