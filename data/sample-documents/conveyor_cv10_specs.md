# Conveyor CV-10 Specifications

## Equipment Data
- Tag: CV-10
- Type: Belt Conveyor
- Length: 120 meters
- Belt Width: 800 mm
- Capacity: 500 tons/hour

## Maintenance Requirements
- Inspect belt tracking weekly to prevent edge damage.
- Check idler rollers for smooth rotation; replace seized rollers immediately to avoid fire hazards.
- Ensure emergency pull cords are functional along the entire length.
