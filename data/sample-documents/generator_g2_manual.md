# Generator G-2 Manual

## Asset Information
- Tag: G-2
- Type: Diesel Backup Generator
- Power Rating: 2 MW
- Voltage: 415 V

## Testing Schedule
- Weekly no-load test for 15 minutes.
- Monthly load-bank test at 80% capacity for 2 hours.
- Annual fuel polishing and filter replacement.

## Common Alerts
- "Low Fuel Pressure": Check primary fuel filter for blockages.
- "Battery Voltage Low": Ensure trickle charger is connected and functioning.
