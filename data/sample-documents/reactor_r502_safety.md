# Reactor R-502 Safety Guidelines

## Asset Identification
- Tag: R-502
- Type: Continuous Stirred-Tank Reactor (CSTR)
- Process: Exothermic Polymerization

## Safety Procedures
- The cooling jacket flow must be established BEFORE introducing reactants.
- In the event of a runaway reaction (temperature > 150°C), the automated deluge system will activate.
- Personnel must not enter the containment area during active processing.
