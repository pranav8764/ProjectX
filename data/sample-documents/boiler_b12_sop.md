# Boiler B-12 Standard Operating Procedure

## Asset Information
- Tag: B-12
- Type: Steam Boiler
- Fuel: Natural Gas
- Operating Pressure: 15 bar

## Startup Procedure
1. Verify water level is within normal range.
2. Ensure all safety valves are calibrated and functional.
3. Initiate purge cycle for 5 minutes.
4. Ignite pilot and monitor flame stability.
5. Gradually increase load to 50%.

## Safety
- Always wear appropriate PPE.
- In case of flame failure, immediately close main gas valve.
